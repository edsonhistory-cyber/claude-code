# Facts in The Last Night Train

The film is fiction. Nothing on screen stands for a real railway, place or event.

- **Stations**: PINE HALT (the departure), ELM HALT, STONE HALT and MILL HALT (passed on the way) and SEA END (the terminus) are invented names. They are not real stations.
- **"For forty years"**: the age of the line is part of the story, not a fact about any railway.
- **The train**: a generic old night train drawn in code (an engine and seven carriages). It is not modelled on a real locomotive.
